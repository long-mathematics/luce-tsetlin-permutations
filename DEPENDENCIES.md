# Mathematical dependency map

The catalog records document-level edges and tests reject cycles. Proof modules are the meaningful unit of review.

## Intended current chain

`mesoscopic-switching` supplies the exact marked swap identity, selected-factor tails, internal root bounds, and free-depth one-/two-root endpoint comparison.

`erdos-turan-order` imports those modules and adds the rate-oriented three-clock estimate and product-to-LCM arithmetic transfer.

`sharp-order-edgeworth` imports the switching/order interfaces. Its polynomial switching, passive-mark Campbell–Stein, and fixed-order factorial-domination modules can be used without importing its nonlinear arithmetic Edgeworth argument.

`functional-erdos-turan` needs the summable switching intensity and dimension-free Poisson–Stein transfer; the count-only CLT needs no arithmetic order or macroscopic PD theorem. The joint order coordinate adds the scalar paper's product-to-LCM estimate.

`total-cycle-edgeworth` will use the polynomial passive core, independent copied omitted count, exact-mean bookkeeping, and fixed-order cycle domination. Its Poisson/Charlier smoothing proof is distinct from the logarithmic-order arithmetic proof.

## Forbidden shortcuts

Neither the historical total-cycle resolution candidate nor a filename marked 'final' is an input. Fixed-dimensional moments alone do not justify growing-dimensional Poisson approximation. Total variation alone does not justify unbounded moment transfer. Macroscopic PD/GEM is not an input to the comparable-rate total-cycle proof. No growing-root or arbitrary-deletion quenched theorem is assumed.
