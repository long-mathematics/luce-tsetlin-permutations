# Profile-dependent constants and a conjectural analytic residue

This is an open-problem note, not an input to any current theorem. The total-cycle paper proves finite-order expansions with exact finite-n coefficients; it does not identify their limits.

For a sampled positive continuous profile f, the earlier candidate proposed the mean shift gamma_E + sum_r(lambda_r(f)-1/r). The historical proof of that shift remains invalidated. The CLT and quantitative total-cycle results do not reinstate it.

An explicitly conjectural stronger target is local uniform convergence near z=1 of

$$
e^{-(z-1)\log n}\mathbb E z^{K_n}
\longrightarrow \frac{e^{A_f(z-1)}}{\Gamma(z)},
\qquad A_f=\sum_{r\ge1}(\lambda_r(f)-1/r).
$$

It would imply mean shift gamma_E+A_f and universal higher factorial-cumulant limits (-1)^{j+1}(j-1)! zeta(j) for j>=2. This is a candidate, not evidence that the limits are universal. Finite-dimensional cycle convergence, PD/GEM convergence, and order-dependent Poisson--Charlier estimates do not by themselves justify differentiating such a limiting analytic residue.

Approximate deterministic centers must respect the exact lattice threshold: replacing the mean inside a fractional-part function can change jump phases, even for a small centering error.
